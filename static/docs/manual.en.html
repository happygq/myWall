● myWall v4.1

Visual disc library: browse the wall, search metadata, upload spine photos, confirm TMDb matches, and adjust placement.

Browse Search Upload & Scan Edit discs Placement Spine boxes Language

Browse your library

Use List for a compact catalog or Folders to group discs by source photo. Select a card to open details. The pin locates a disc on the wall when position data exists. Drag the sidebar or detail panel handles to peek at the wall behind panels.

Search and filters

Search matches titles, directors, and cast. Filter by genre, year, or preference. Open the genre-cloud icon for a visual hot-word filter — click a word to filter; drag words to rearrange.

Upload & Scan

Unlock editing, expand Tools, then choose Upload & Scan. Drop spine close-up photos (JPG/PNG/WebP). Existing photos can be re-analyzed or sent through Stage2 after spine boxes are saved.

  • Analyze — vision/OCR reads spine text.
  • Match — pick TMDb candidates; reject bad hits; calibrate spine or search manually.
  • Placement — drag photo boxes on the wall background (optional).

Read-only, manual cards, and editing

myWall starts read-only. Click the lock next to the version to enable editing. Add Disc creates a card without Stage2. Edit opens title/year/TMDb fields with optional source-photo spine preview. Complete Posters fills missing posters from existing TMDb IDs without changing titles or boxes.

Wall placement

After batch upload, mark each close-up’s position on the wall. In folder view, click a group thumbnail to adjust a single close-up (Adjust close-up on wall). Saved placement updates disc markers on the main wall.

Spine boxes

Stage2 and visual compare need accurate spine regions. In upload or image management, select one photo and open Edit spine boxes. Drag boxes on the source image, then save to the server. Keyboard shortcuts: A add, C copy, Delete remove, S save.

Language

Choose English or 简体中文 from the Language menu. UI strings reload immediately; open modals refresh on switch. Preference is stored in localStorage (mywall.uiLocale).
