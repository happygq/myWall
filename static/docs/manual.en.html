● myWall v4.1

Your visual disc library: browse the wall, search the catalog, inspect metadata, and maintain recognition results.

BrowseSearchEditLanguage

Browse your library

Use List for a compact catalog or Folders to group discs by source photo. Select a card to open its details. The pin action locates a disc on the wall when position data is available.

Search and filters

Search matches titles, directors, and cast. Narrow results by genre, year, or preference. The genre-cloud button opens a visual hot-word filter.

Read-only and editing

myWall starts in read-only mode. Use the lock beside the version number to enable editing. The Tools tray then provides upload, manual card creation, image management, and poster completion.

Language

Choose English or 简体中文 from the Language menu. The choice is saved in localStorage. Japanese and Korean UI dictionaries are planned for a later milestone.
